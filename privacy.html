Privacy Policy

NOIR STRANDS respects your privacy. This page explains how information submitted through our website may be used.

Information we collect

When you submit a creator application, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, social media username, application responses, and questions.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review creator applications, communicate with applicants, arrange product shipping when applicable, and provide customer or creator support.

Third parties

Application submissions are processed through our form service provider, FormSubmit. Information is shared with that provider only as necessary to process the submission.

Data choices

Please do not submit information that is not requested or necessary for your application. If you have a question about information you submitted, contact NOIR STRANDS at noirstrands5@gmail.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated as our website or processes change.
